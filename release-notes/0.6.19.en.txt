Herdline 0.6.19 — Send says what to do next

When Chat can't send to OMP because its input box isn't readable on the phone, the message now tells you what to do, then says nothing was sent:

- If OMP's input box isn't drawn in the pane, open Terminal and check OMP is at its prompt.
- If something is drawn over the box, close it in Terminal.
- If the top of the box isn't on screen, hide the keyboard or use a smaller text size.
- If the box can't be read, check it in Terminal.

Then send again. Your text stays in the composer.
