Herdline 0.2.1 — Terminal navigation hotfix.

Fixes Terminal navigation in 0.2.0: with Terminal on screen, choosing a tab chip, Prev or Next, a Tabs grid or list card, a pane or Whole tab in the pane picker, or a workspace could leave the desktop and the screen where they were when the chosen pane would read in Chat. A choice made in Terminal now moves Herdr's shared focus to it. In Chat, choosing a pane that reads in Chat still changes only what this phone reads and does not focus the desktop. Notifications and deep links keep their 0.2.0 behaviour.

No other changes. Branding, package, signer, delivery identity, receipts, uncertain holds and send guards are unchanged.

Verification: developer checks only, no runtime QA. Terminal and Chat navigation on a device are unverified.
