Herdline 0.6.18 — Your pane stays on screen

When a split layout is taller than your phone's screen, the terminal now shows the pane you are working in. Before, if the cursor was in another pane, the screen could show only that other pane and none of yours.

If the cursor is in your pane, the screen keeps the cursor's row in view, as before. If any row of your pane is already on screen, nothing moves. A pane taller than the screen shows its top rows.
