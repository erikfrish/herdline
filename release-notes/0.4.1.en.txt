Herdline 0.4.1 — private form-display hotfix

A choice group with a fieldset legend no longer repeats its internal HTML field name above the radio buttons, checkboxes or select. Explicit field labels, ungrouped fields and required indicators remain visible.

When a question blocks Chat Send, the notice now says it can be answered in Chat when controls are available, or in Terminal. Sending still refuses while a dialog is open; no approval or retry is automatic.

The 0.4.0 exact-artifact QA was conditional: Send, photo-only admission, the real steering queue, withdrawal, local Chat focus/geometry, uncertain-send restart and form placement passed with a local mock provider. The legend/name duplication failed at S3. Non-submittable-dialog runtime coverage and Terminal password scrollback were not verified; form selections reset after process death. The recovery scenario proved no replay, not loss of an acknowledgement after already-observed admission. This hotfix does not change those contracts or claim LLM consumption.

Verification of this new APK is recorded separately. Personal OMP is not updated automatically by installing the app.
