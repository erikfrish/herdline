Herdline 0.6.17 — Keep your place

Reading why Chat's saved history couldn't refresh no longer takes focus from the field you were typing in. Before, closing the More dialog moved focus to the More button. Now nothing moves focus when the dialog closes.

The More dialog also stays open, with the same words, if Chat manages to refresh while you read it. Switching to another pane or agent closes it, so one pane's reason never shows over another's.
